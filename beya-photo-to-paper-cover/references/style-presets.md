# Five Beya Style Presets

Use exactly one preset. The selected style should meaningfully change the illustration half's composition, texture, color behavior, line language, and typography mood.

## Style 1: Quiet Geometry Poster

Inspired by the mountain cabin and pine-cow demo: a clean minimalist editorial poster with flat geometric landforms and rough paper-print texture. This preset must not become a realistic landscape painting.

- Composition: default photo on top, illustration below. Use a small low horizontal stage across the lower third of the illustration half, with very large warm paper margins above and below. In illustration-only mode, still keep the scene as a compact vignette rather than filling the page. Subject scale: 10-22% of the full canvas, or 12-24% of the illustration half.
- Visual extraction: convert mountains, buildings, roads, fields, trees, furniture, or objects into simplified block silhouettes. Preserve the iconic horizon, slope, single main object, and calm spatial distance. For landscape photos, reduce the whole scene to 3-6 pieces: one ground plane, one background mass, one path/line element, one tiny subject group, and at most one distant cue.
- Marks: flat acrylic and dry screenprint-like shapes with visible broken pigment, rough edges, paper bleed, and imperfect registration; very little linework. Use angular but softened polygons, clean overlaps, and subtle shadow planes. Avoid smooth watercolor realism.
- Palette: 3-5 muted editorial colors. Favor lichen green, blue-gray, slate, soft olive, chalk cream, and one restrained dark accent. Keep contrast low but clear.
- Texture: matte acrylic on ivory poster paper, obvious paper tooth, dry pigment gaps, imperfect edges, no glossy gradients.
- Signature: very small handwritten signature in the lower-right corner, muted olive-gray ink, with tiny handwritten `2026` nearby. For `beya`, use the extra-small long-tail dot signature.
- Avoid: realistic watercolor/photo redraw, full-frame scenic painting, detailed trees, dense grass strokes, too many fence posts or buildings, soft photographic shading, decorative scenery.

Prompt insert:

```text
Render the illustration half as a quiet minimalist geometric paper-print poster, not a realistic painting. Build the scene from a few flat acrylic planes, softened polygon silhouettes, rough dry pigment, and calm horizontal composition. Keep the main motif small, low, and spacious, with large ivory paper margins. For landscape photos, reduce the whole scene to 3-6 essential components only: a ground plane, a background mass, a path or horizon line, a tiny subject group, and one distant cue if needed. Use 3-5 premium muted colors such as lichen green, blue-gray, slate, soft olive, chalk cream, and one restrained dark accent. Use almost no linework; let shape, proportion, rough texture, and negative space carry recognition. The result should feel like an expensive independent art-book cover or paper plate: precise, still, airy, understated, and clearly abstracted from the photo.
```

## Style 2: Still Object Collage

Inspired by the fountain demo: a deconstructed object collage with quiet stone, water, and garden tones.

- Composition: default photo on top, illustration below. Place the core object cluster near the center, surrounded by broad blank paper. Subject scale: 15-28% of the illustration half.
- Visual extraction: reduce architecture, food, plants, animals, or objects into a balanced still-life arrangement. Choose 3-6 essential parts and separate them into calm stacked forms.
- Marks: soft-edged paper collage blocks, translucent gouache stains, charcoal-black anchor shape, minimal thin line accents.
- Palette: 4-6 mineral colors. Favor warm stone, smoked olive, oxidized green, tea beige, bone white, charcoal, and muted bronze.
- Texture: handmade paper, dry pigment, rubbed edges, slightly uneven opacity, quiet archival print feeling.
- Signature: very small handwritten signature in the lower-right corner, muted olive-gray ink, with tiny handwritten `2026` nearby. For `beya`, use the extra-small long-tail dot signature.
- Avoid: cute illustration, high saturation, clean vector geometry, postcard hatching, busy environmental detail.

Prompt insert:

```text
Render the illustration half as a quiet deconstructed still-object collage. Break the source photo into 3-6 essential shapes and rebuild them as a centered, balanced object cluster on warm handmade paper. Use soft-edged collage blocks, translucent gouache stains, one charcoal-black anchor shape, and a few thin line accents. Use a refined mineral palette: warm stone, smoked olive, oxidized green, tea beige, bone white, charcoal, and muted bronze. The result should feel like an archival art-book plate: calm, sculptural, intelligent, and expensive.
```

## Style 3: Wax Pencil Keepsake

Inspired by the cake demo: a warm wax-pencil and notebook sketch with soft dessert-like color.

- Composition: illustration may appear on top and the real photo below if it better matches the chosen source; otherwise keep photo on top. Put the illustrated subject on a soft rectangular wash patch with ample paper around it. Subject scale: 20-35% of the illustration half.
- Visual extraction: best for food, small objects, table scenes, pets, flowers, toys, souvenirs, and intimate details. Preserve the object's contour, cut, gesture, or distinctive top surface.
- Marks: graphite underdrawing, uneven wax-pencil scribble, dry pastel grain, small broken contour lines, visible hand pressure.
- Palette: 4-6 warm refined colors. Favor butter yellow, pale pistachio, honey ochre, oat beige, milk white, dusty apricot, and a small olive or cocoa accent.
- Texture: cream notebook paper, wax bloom, scumbled fill, rubbed pencil, unfinished edges.
- Signature: very small handwritten signature in the lower-right corner, very pale muted olive-gray pencil/ink, with tiny handwritten `2026` nearby. For `beya`, use the extra-small long-tail dot signature.
- Avoid: sharp vector planes, dark poster contrast, hard ink print, over-realistic rendering, childish doodles.

Prompt insert:

```text
Render the illustration half as a warm wax-pencil keepsake sketch on cream notebook paper. Place the subject on a pale rectangular wash patch with generous blank space. Use graphite underdrawing, broken pencil contours, uneven wax-pencil scribbles, dry pastel grain, and visible hand pressure. Use a premium soft palette: butter yellow, pale pistachio, honey ochre, oat beige, milk white, dusty apricot, with one small olive or cocoa accent. The result should feel intimate, handmade, editorial, and quietly luxurious, like a preserved page from a personal visual diary.
```

## Style 4: Coastal Postmark Print

Inspired by the red lighthouse demo: a vintage travel postcard print with ink hatching and one strong accent color.

- Composition: default photo on top, illustration below. Place the illustrated landmark or subject slightly right of center or low-center, like a printed postcard vignette on blank cream stock. Subject scale: 18-32% of the illustration half.
- Visual extraction: best for travel scenes, architecture, vehicles, coastlines, bridges, monuments, city corners, signs, and strong silhouettes. Preserve the icon, base structure, foreground shape, and horizon cue.
- Marks: dark ink outlines, dry screenprint texture, engraving-like hatch marks, tiny misregistration, limited flat spot colors.
- Palette: 3-5 colors. Favor naval blue ink, aged cream, weathered gray, faded red or signal orange, and one desaturated source-derived accent.
- Texture: vintage postcard paper, letterpress grain, stamped ink, slight fading, tactile print edges.
- Signature: very small handwritten signature in the lower-right corner, muted olive-gray or ink-wash gray, with tiny handwritten `2026` nearby. For `beya`, use the extra-small long-tail dot signature.
- Avoid: watercolor washbook softness, sweet pastel crayon, pure geometric poster, cute mascot look, large decorative type.

Prompt insert:

```text
Render the illustration half as a refined vintage travel postcard print. Rebuild the source as an iconic printed vignette with dark ink outlines, dry screenprint grain, engraving-like hatching, tiny misregistration, and limited flat spot colors. Use a premium palette: naval blue ink, aged cream, weathered gray, faded red or signal orange, and one muted source-derived accent. Keep the subject compact with large blank cream stock around it. The result should feel collected, graphic, coastal, literary, and beautifully printed.
```

## Style 5: Pastoral Washbook

Inspired by the sheep demo: a soft watercolor/gouache picture-book page with tender natural detail.

- Composition: default photo on top, illustration below. Place one gentle subject or small group in the center with a few environmental hints: grass, fence, hill, sun dot, shadow patch, or small plants. Subject scale: 16-30% of the illustration half.
- Visual extraction: best for animals, people in quiet motion, gardens, countryside, domestic moments, nature scenes, and soft emotional photos. Preserve the posture, companionship, direction of gaze, and tender mood.
- Marks: translucent watercolor washes, soft gouache body color, delicate pencil contours, sparse grass strokes, fading edges, visible paper tooth.
- Palette: 4-6 quiet natural colors. Favor moss green, flax, wool white, warm gray, pale peach sun, dried grass yellow, and soft sage.
- Texture: warm watercolor paper, feathered pigment, granulation, dry-brush grass, subtle handmade unevenness.
- Signature: very small handwritten signature in the lower-right corner, pale gray-brown or muted olive-gray, with tiny handwritten `2026` nearby. For `beya`, use the extra-small long-tail dot signature.
- Avoid: hard geometric simplification, heavy ink hatching, high contrast poster color, overly cute cartoon animals, dense landscape painting.

Prompt insert:

```text
Render the illustration half as a tender pastoral washbook page. Use translucent watercolor washes, soft gouache body color, delicate pencil contours, sparse grass strokes, fading edges, and visible warm paper tooth. Keep one gentle subject or small group centered with only a few environmental hints such as grass, fence, hill, sun dot, shadow patch, or tiny plants. Use a premium natural palette: moss green, flax, wool white, warm gray, pale peach sun, dried grass yellow, and soft sage. The result should feel poetic, quiet, refined, and emotionally warm without becoming childish.
```
